Questions

What Daikonbun is — and isn't.

What is Daikonbun?
A self-hosted workspace where your AI coding agents work under review. Your own agent drives the board over MCP, refining, planning, and building, and every task passes an independent review gate before it merges, with a signed record of exactly how it was done. Daikonbun holds no model keys; your agent does the work on your own subscription.
How do my agents know how to operate it?
It speaks MCP. Point any MCP client (Claude Code, Cursor, or your own) at the board, and on connect the server tells it the whole workflow: the stages, the gates, and how to move a card. There's no plugin to write and no prompt to craft; your agent knows how to run the board out of the box.
Why not just my agent + GitHub PR review?
If a human reads every pull request, GitHub is enough. Daikonbun is for when your agents open more changes than anyone actually reviews. It makes review structural and agent-aware: an independent gate on every task the server won't let a card skip, and a record of which agent did what, not just "a human approved a PR." GitHub is your process until agents outrun hand-review; that's the line this is built for.
Does it run the agents? Do I pay per token?
No. You bring your own agent (Claude Code, Cursor, or your own) and it drives the board on your subscription. Daikonbun holds no model credentials and makes no model calls, so there's nothing to meter and no key to hand over.
Isn't this just a kanban board?
A board tracks work. Daikonbun governs it: a review gate on every task, enforced by the server, and a signed record of how the work was done. The columns aren't the product; the review and the record are.
Is it really self-hosted?
Yes. One docker compose up runs the board and its database on your own machine. Your data, your API keys, your infrastructure. A hosted option may come later, but self-host is the primary product.
Which AI models does it use?
It's provider-agnostic. Each agent role maps to a model through a routing table you control, so you bring your own LLM and route stronger models to planning and review, cheaper ones to mechanical steps.
Is my data private?
Because Daikonbun is self-hosted, your tasks, credentials, and work records stay on your machine. Credentials are encrypted at rest and never returned in plaintext by any API.
Is it available yet, and is it free?
Daikonbun is in beta: working software, pre-1.0. Join early access to self-host it as access opens up. It's free for noncommercial self-host, with a per-seat commercial license for paid use.
Wait — why "Daikonbun"?
Say it fast: daikon-bun. Yes — die, kanban. But the joke was never that we built a prettier board. Agents made "track the work" the easy part; the hard part is trusting what they shipped. So Daikonbun isn't a better board. It's the review and the record around your agents' work.
